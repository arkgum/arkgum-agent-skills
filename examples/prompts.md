# Example prompts

## Research to page

```text
Use $arkgum-research-to-page to research the current alternatives to traditional
RAG for a personal knowledge base. Produce an evidence-led page brief and a
builder prompt for a technical audience. Do not invent traffic or SEO metrics.
```

```text
Use $arkgum-research-to-page to turn this product idea into a source-grounded
landing-page specification. Keep unknown business inputs as placeholders and
show me the selected opportunity before handoff.
```
